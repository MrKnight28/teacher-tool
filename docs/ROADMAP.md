# Roadmap

Dates in `packs/security-plus-sy0-701/pack.yaml` are examples. Set your own exam date, any pause, and the monthly spend cap in setup or on the admin page.

Already in the app: a daily lesson, spoken teaching, exam-style questions marked on the server, progress by objective, reminders, a notebook, and optional WhatsApp and Tailscale.

Not built yet:

- Homework that is assigned and marked. A lesson plan can store a homework task. Nothing hands it out.
- Timed mini-mocks.
- Hands-free listening. The microphone works while you hold the button.
